# Code of Conduct / 行为准则

Anbot is a project for people who want a reliable personal agent. Its repository, issues, pull requests, discussions and other community spaces should stay useful to that work.

Anbot 是一个做可靠个人智能体的项目。它的仓库、issue、pull request、讨论区和其他社区空间，都应该服务于这件事。

## Expected behavior / 期望的行为

- Be direct and technical. Criticize the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, but ask before assuming a motive.
- Keep reports reproducible: include the version, the steps and the evidence you have.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's keys, tokens, files, chats, home directory or personal data.
- Accept a maintainer's decision on scope, design and release, even when you disagree. Argue with the reasoning; do not reopen the same decision without new evidence.

- 直接、技术化地讨论。批评工作，不批评人。
- 先假定对方没有恶意；要判断动机，先问。
- 报告要可复现：带上版本、步骤和你掌握的证据。
- 尊重隐私。不要公开别人的密钥、token、文件、聊天、家目录或个人信息。
- 尊重维护者对范围、设计和发布的决定，即使你不同意。可以争论理由；没有新证据不要反复重开同一个决定。

## Unacceptable behavior / 不可接受的行为

- Insults, harassment, threats, intimidation or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory language or attacks based on gender, age, disability, ethnicity, nationality, religion, sexual orientation or appearance.
- Sexual content or unwelcome sexual attention.
- Publishing private information without permission, including doxxing.
- Spam, vote manipulation, or opening issues and pull requests in bad faith.
- Pressuring someone to disclose secrets or to run untrusted code against a system they do not own.

- 侮辱、骚扰、威胁、恐吓或持续扰乱讨论。
- 基于性别、年龄、残障、民族、国籍、宗教、性取向或外貌的歧视性言论或攻击。
- 色情内容或不受欢迎的性关注。
- 未经允许公开私人信息，包括人肉搜索。
- 垃圾信息、刷票，或恶意提交 issue 和 pull request。
- 施压他人泄露密钥，或在他人不拥有的系统上运行不可信代码。

## Scope / 适用范围

This code applies in repository spaces and when someone represents Anbot in another space. It also applies to private communication that starts from the repository.

本准则适用于仓库空间，也适用于有人以 Anbot 名义在其他空间活动时；同样适用于从仓库开始的私下沟通。

## Reporting / 举报

Do not open a public issue for a conduct problem. Contact the repository owner privately on GitHub: [@rml4791lol-lang](https://github.com/rml4791lol-lang). If you cannot reach them, use GitHub's **Report abuse** flow.

请不要为行为问题开公开 issue。在 GitHub 上私下联系仓库所有者：[@rml4791lol-lang](https://github.com/rml4791lol-lang)。如果联系不上，使用 GitHub 的 **Report abuse** 流程。

Please include what happened, where it happened, links or screenshots if safe, and what outcome you are asking for. Remove keys, tokens, chat content and personal data before sending.

请说明发生了什么、发生在哪里、安全的链接或截图，以及你希望的处理结果。发送前删除密钥、token、聊天内容和个人信息。

## Enforcement / 执行

The maintainer reviews reports and may remove content, warn, limit participation or block an account. The response depends on the behavior, its impact and whether it repeats. Reports are handled privately; do not retaliate against someone who reports in good faith.

维护者会审阅举报，并可能删除内容、警告、限制参与或封禁账号。处理方式取决于行为、影响和是否重复。举报会私下处理；不要报复善意举报的人。
